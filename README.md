# Tars SDR Assignment — Behavioral Health Customer Acquisition Territory

## Submission structure

- **Account Research** — 20 account dossiers covering public company facts, observed digital journey, customer-acquisition relevance, Tars hypothesis, and research source.
- **Contacts** — two outreach contacts per account (40 contacts total), with title, role-fit rationale, primary angle, and verification note.
- **Email Sequences** — a five-step cold-email sequence for every contact (200 touches total).
- **LinkedIn Sequences** — a five-step LinkedIn sequence for every contact (200 touches total).
- **Call Scripts** — a personalized cold-call script for every contact (40 scripts total).
- **Sources** — account-level public research sources used in the workbook.

## Messaging guardrails

Every sequence positions Tars for **customer acquisition**, not clinical assessment or care delivery: answer approved questions, identify and qualify intent, reduce pre-admissions friction, route or book the correct next step, and measure conversion.

Tars is not positioned as a system for diagnosis, treatment recommendations, crisis intervention, risk assessment, or level-of-care decisions. Those responsibilities remain with the organization’s qualified clinical and admissions teams. Any deployment should use approved knowledge, explicit escalation paths, and the organization’s privacy, compliance, security, and clinical-review processes.
