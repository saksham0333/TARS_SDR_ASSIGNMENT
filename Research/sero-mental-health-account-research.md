## What Sero does

Sero Mental Health is a privately held behavioral-health platform founded in 2021 and headquartered in Alachua, Florida. It develops and operates a nationwide network of residential and outpatient treatment centers across mental health, addiction/substance use, eating disorders, and co-occurring conditions. Its portfolio includes center brands such as Alta Mira Recovery Programs, Wings Recovery, Breathe Life Healing Centers, Bridges to Recovery, BrightQuest, Simply Bee, SunCloud Health, and the Center for the Treatment of Anxiety and Mood Disorders.

## Scale and market position

- **501–1,000 employees** on LinkedIn; **2,800+ LinkedIn followers**.
- A nationwide, multi-brand network spanning several levels of care and patient-acuity needs.
- Sero’s 2026 public messaging emphasizes **de novo growth**, expanded access, and stronger pathways to care.

## Customer experience today

### Observed consumer journey

1. A prospective patient or family member reaches Sero’s corporate site and sees individual treatment-center brands, their locations, phone numbers, and links to each brand’s website.
2. They select a center based on condition, location, level of care, or insurance availability.
3. They can call a center directly, continue to the center’s site, or use Sero’s HIPAA-hosted corporate contact form.
4. The detailed admissions, insurance, and clinical-fit conversation occurs within a chosen center’s workflow.

### Strengths and friction

- The network clearly exposes several specialized programs and direct center contact paths.
- A visitor who is uncertain about the right brand, condition program, geography, insurance, or level of care must navigate a portfolio of distinct sites and numbers before reaching the correct admissions path.
- That is an observation from Sero’s public web experience—not a claim about internal performance.

## Why Sero should care about Tars

Tars can provide a **strictly non-clinical, governed** conversational front door across the portfolio:

- Answer approved questions about center locations, programs, insurance availability, and admissions process.
- Gather minimal routing context: location/state, care interest, age group where appropriate, insurance/self-pay, preferred contact method, and urgency indicator.
- Direct the visitor to the appropriate existing center/admissions workflow, without making clinical recommendations or eligibility decisions.
- Preserve the visitor’s context in a structured handoff so admissions teams do not repeat basic discovery.
- Apply prominent crisis/emergency escalation rules and human handoff where the conversation exceeds approved scope.

This is relevant to both contacts because the business is expanding access across a multi-brand network while Daniel’s stated remit includes growth and patient experience, and Kristen is responsible for the network’s strategy and operations.
