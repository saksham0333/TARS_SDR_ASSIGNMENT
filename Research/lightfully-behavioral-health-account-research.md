## What Lightfully does

Lightfully Behavioral Health is a privately held primary mental-health provider founded in 2021 and headquartered in Thousand Oaks, California. It provides personalized, evidence-based care for adults and teens, including residential treatment, partial hospitalization (PHP), intensive outpatient (IOP), and virtual services. Its public materials position its clinical approach around process-based therapy and measurable results.

## Customer experience today

### Observed admissions journey

1. A visitor reaches Lightfully’s program, condition, location, or educational content pages and is invited to call or connect with the Admissions Concierge Team.
2. An admissions counselor gathers initial information and answers questions.
3. With consent, the team verifies insurance and reviews benefits; Lightfully works with many commercial plans, single-case agreements, and out-of-network benefits, but does not accept Medicare.
4. A masters-level clinician conducts a phone assessment, then the clinical team determines its recommendation and, after clearance, admissions schedules the start date.

### Strengths and opportunity

- The public journey is warm and transparent, with a well-described human admissions process and a strong insurance explanation.
- A person experiencing anxiety or supporting a loved one may have basic pre-admissions questions about program type, age group, location, insurance, virtual versus in-person options, or how the process works before they feel ready to call.
- A governed conversation could resolve approved non-clinical questions immediately and preserve context for the existing admissions counselor. This is an inference from public pages, not a claim about Lightfully’s internal performance.

## Why Lightfully should care about Tars

Tars can support a **strictly non-clinical, governed** first conversation that complements Lightfully’s Admissions Concierge model:

- Answer approved questions about programs, locations, insurance process, and admissions steps.
- Collect only minimal context—adult/teen pathway, general program interest, location, insurance/self-pay, and preferred contact method.
- Route into the current admissions-contact workflow with a concise summary for the counselor.
- Escalate quickly to human and emergency/crisis pathways; never diagnose, assess clinical suitability, or recommend treatment.

The goal is not to substitute for Lightfully’s compassionate admissions or clinical assessment. It is to make the first digital moment more responsive and make the human handoff better informed.
