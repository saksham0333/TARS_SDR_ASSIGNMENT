## What Quartet does

Quartet is a value-based behavioral-health enablement and delivery solution now owned by NeuroFlow. It partners with health plans, health systems, and community mental-health centers to identify people who need behavioral care and connect them to high-quality providers, including its own medical group. Its Care Connections model combines intake, matching, care navigation, and provider-network access.

## Scale and market position

- Trusted partner to health plans, health systems, and community mental-health centers in **39 states**, according to Quartet’s current site.
- Current/legacy Quartet materials report **1.1M+ members engaged**, **20,000 providers**, and an average match to care within **48 hours**; those claims are historical marketing figures and should be revalidated before using externally.
- Quartet was acquired by NeuroFlow in 2025. NeuroFlow’s platform focuses on integrated behavioral-health support for healthcare organizations, creating an enterprise behavioral-health technology and services combination.

## Customer experience today

### Observed patient and partner journey

1. On Quartet’s “Get care” page, a person can select state and insurance information to see whether direct sign-up is available.
2. If their plan/area is not supported, the site may direct them to a partner or collect name, email, ZIP, state, and insurance to notify them when access expands.
3. Eligible patients can enter a care journey; Quartet’s published model combines matching and Care Navigation support.
4. Enterprise buyers can access partner/provider resources and contact Quartet by phone or web inquiry.

### Strengths and opportunity

- Quartet already has a mature care-navigation and matching proposition. Tars should **not** be positioned as a replacement for clinical matching, Care Navigators, or its care-delivery model.
- The enterprise website must serve a complex audience—health plans, health systems, community organizations, referring providers, and potential patients—with questions about integration, deployment, eligibility, and programs.
- Tars can add value as a governed conversational acquisition layer: answer approved commercial/product questions, identify buyer type and interest, capture a qualified inquiry, and route it to the appropriate growth or partner team. On the patient side, it can only handle approved logistical information and route to Quartet’s existing care journey.

## Why Quartet/NeuroFlow should care about Tars

- Convert more high-intent enterprise web visitors into qualified sales conversations instead of generic contact forms.
- Answer approved first-party questions about partner types, deployment model, service footprint, and how to begin an evaluation.
- Capture structured fields—organization type, population/market, use case, timeline, and contact details—and route them to the right commercial owner or CRM.
- Preserve strict non-clinical boundaries for any patient-facing interaction: no diagnosis, care matching, or eligibility decision; route to Quartet’s current model, human teams, and emergency resources.
