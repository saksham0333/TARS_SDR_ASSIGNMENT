## What Newport Healthcare does

Newport Healthcare is a private, nationwide behavioral-health provider for children, teens, young adults, and families. It provides residential treatment, partial hospitalization (PHP), intensive outpatient (IOP), and—in its PrairieCare division in Minnesota—inpatient psychiatric care. Its divisions include Newport Academy, Newport Institute, Center for Families, and PrairieCare.

## Size and footprint

- LinkedIn lists Newport at **1,001–5,000 employees** and shows roughly **1,450 visible employees**.
- Newport serves children ages **7–11**, teens **12–18**, and young adults **18–35**; PrairieCare supports people of all ages.
- Newport says it has residential and outpatient locations nationwide. A November 2024 company release cited **800+ beds** and **21 outpatient programs** at that time.
- Newport’s FAQ says about **98–99%** of clients use insurance to pay for care.

## Customer experience today

### Family / prospective-client journey

1. A parent, family member, young adult, or referring professional explores program, condition, age, location, and insurance information.
2. They can call a 24/7 admissions line or submit a two-step contact form.
3. The form asks for contact information, the visitor’s relationship to the prospective client, insurance, prospective-client age, state/ZIP, and optional details.
4. Admissions guides callers through clinical and medical screenings, insurance verification, and a virtual clinical assessment.
5. If Newport is a fit, an Admissions Coordinator manages logistics for admission.

### Observed strengths

- 24/7 human admissions support and a prominent contact path.
- A full continuum of care, multiple brands/divisions, and insurance-verification support.
- Clearly differentiated paths for parents/families, prospective clients, and professionals.

### Likely friction worth validating

These are public-site observations, not assertions of operational problems:

- Families may arrive with multi-part, urgent but non-clinical questions: suitable program, age eligibility, location, insurance, level of care, and what happens next.
- A static form captures some context but does not answer questions in the moment or help a visitor choose the correct existing pathway.
- Newport’s admissions process necessarily includes human clinical assessment. The opportunity is therefore the **pre-admissions, non-clinical** conversation—not clinical triage or a treatment recommendation.

## Why Newport should care about Tars

Tars can provide a governed AI front door for approved, non-clinical information across web, SMS, email, or other supported channels:

- Answer approved questions about programs, ages served, locations, payment/insurance process, and next steps.
- Collect only the operational context needed to route a visitor: relationship to client, age band, state/ZIP, program interest, insurance category, and preferred follow-up.
- Guide the visitor to Newport’s existing 24/7 admissions, form, professional-referral, or insurance workflow.
- Pass a concise conversation summary so an admissions team need not repeat basic discovery.
- Escalate immediately to Newport’s human admissions and emergency/crisis instructions where appropriate; do not offer clinical advice, diagnosis, or level-of-care determinations.

For Kristin, the case is a growth-and-experience hypothesis: preserve qualified family and referral intent while maintaining a compassionate, safe handoff to admissions. Any deployment should be reviewed by Newport’s clinical, privacy, compliance, and security teams, with appropriate HIPAA safeguards where applicable.
