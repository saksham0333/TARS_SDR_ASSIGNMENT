## What Discovery does

Discovery Behavioral Health (DBH) is a privately held, nationwide behavioral-health provider headquartered in Irvine, California. It serves children, adolescents, and adults with mental-health, eating-disorder, substance-use/addiction, self-harm, attachment, mood, and anxiety needs across residential, partial-hospitalization, intensive-outpatient, online, and detox programs.

## Scale and market position

- **1,001–5,000 employees** on LinkedIn.
- **130+ programs nationwide**, according to the company website; a current recruiting page states **155+ locations, 12 brands, in 16 states**.
- DBH reports **30,000+ patient outcomes** within its measurement-based-care learning health system.
- In July 2026, DBH announced new majority ownership managed by HPS Investment Partners and a new CEO, presenting this as a financially strengthened new chapter for investment in programs, teams, patients, and families.

## Customer experience today

### Observed consumer journey

1. A prospective patient or family member reaches DBH’s corporate site and can browse treatment categories and a broad geographic/brand center finder.
2. The finder asks the visitor to select a state, then a brand, to see locations; callers can also use a central information number.
3. The portfolio spans mental health, eating disorders, and substance use across different ages, locations, brands, levels of care, and payment options.
4. DBH promotes in-network, out-of-network, and private-pay options; its programs include long-term post-discharge support.

### Strengths and friction

- DBH has deep clinical differentiation and a broad continuum of care, backed by measurement-based outcomes.
- A visitor with a multi-part question—condition, age, state, level of care, insurance, and timing—must translate that need into a state → brand → location journey before reaching the appropriate admission team.
- This is an inference from DBH’s public site, not a claim about internal admission performance.

## Why DBH should care about Tars

Tars can add a **strictly non-clinical, governed** digital front door across DBH’s multi-brand network:

- Answer approved questions about program types, locations, insurance/payment pathways, and admissions process.
- Collect only routing-relevant context: general care interest, age group where appropriate, state/ZIP, insurance/self-pay, and contact preference.
- Route to the correct existing brand, location, or admissions workflow with a structured summary.
- Provide clear human, emergency, and crisis escalation paths; do not diagnose, recommend clinical treatment, or determine eligibility.

For DBH, the business case is to make its differentiated continuum easier to access and make every admissions handoff more informed—while respecting the safety, privacy, and clinical boundaries of behavioral health.
