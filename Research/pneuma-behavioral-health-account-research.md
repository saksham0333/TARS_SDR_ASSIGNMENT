## What Pneuma does

Pneuma Behavioral Health is a management-services and support organization for clinician-owned behavioral-health practices and facilities. It provides centralized operational, clinical, and strategic support—including billing, compliance, EHR/telehealth implementation, credentialing, recruiting, marketing, analytics, service-line expansion, and revenue optimization—so affiliated practices can retain their identity and focus on patient care. Clinical services are delivered by independently owned and operated affiliated professional entities.

## Scale and market position

- Privately held and headquartered in the Charlotte, North Carolina area; current public materials cite an operating presence in **North Carolina, South Carolina, and Florida**.
- Pneuma targets clinician-owned organizations from solo providers through multi-site groups, supporting outpatient care and expansion into PHP, IOP, testing, medication management, and substance-use services.
- Public messaging emphasizes growth through partnerships, mergers/acquisitions, and organic expansion.
- Company-size data is inconsistent in public directories; use its stated multi-state footprint and partnership model rather than an unverified employee count.

## Customer experience today

### Observed partnership journey

1. A practice owner or behavioral-health leader arrives at Pneuma’s website to evaluate a partnership.
2. The site explains operational excellence, growth support, technology, and its clinician-centered model through lengthy service and FAQ content.
3. A prospective partner can use “Explore Partnership,” “Start the Conversation,” “Get Started,” or a general contact form.
4. Pneuma’s stated approach is to begin with a low-pressure conversation, understand goals/challenges, develop a partnership plan, and then support integration and ongoing growth.

### Strengths and opportunity

- Pneuma’s positioning answers an important fear directly: practices keep their clinical identity, culture, and approach.
- A prospective partner may still need to reconcile many variables before completing a form: practice size, geography, current pain point, desired service expansion, ownership/identity concerns, EHR/revenue-cycle needs, and partnership timing.
- A governed B2B conversation can make this complex evaluation easier, qualify the opportunity, and provide the growth team with structured context. This is an inference from public pages, not a claim about Pneuma’s internal process.

## Why Pneuma should care about Tars

Tars can operate as a governed partnership-acquisition agent:

- Answer approved questions about Pneuma’s support model, practice independence, service lines, growth process, and general geographic focus.
- Qualify prospects by practice type, multi-site/solo status, state, service lines, operational challenge, growth goals, and timeline.
- Route the inquiry into Pneuma’s current partnership-development workflow or CRM with a concise summary.
- Reduce generic contact-form leads while protecting a sensitive, trust-based sale; do not make financial, legal, clinical, or transaction commitments.
