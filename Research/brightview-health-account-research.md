## What BrightView does

BrightView is a privately held outpatient addiction-treatment provider founded in 2015 and headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio. Its local centers provide medication-assisted treatment (MAT), individual counseling, group therapy, and social support for substance use disorders.

## Scale and market position

- **1,001–5,000 employees** on LinkedIn, with approximately **990 employees shown** on the company profile.
- Serves **Ohio, Kentucky, Virginia, Maryland, and North Carolina** through a multi-state outpatient-center footprint.
- Positions around accessible treatment: 24/7 phone response, weekday walk-ins, online scheduling, and same-day or next-day appointments; BrightView says most patients begin care within hours of first contact.

## Customer experience today

### Observed consumer journey

1. A patient or loved one can search for a nearby location by state/city, call a central number, schedule online, or walk in on weekdays.
2. The site clearly explains treatment, insurance, costs, and what to expect during the first days of care.
3. The admissions path promotes same-day/next-day access and begins with an assessment, followed by a personalized clinical treatment plan.
4. Insurance verification and financial-assistance questions can be handled by BrightView’s team; the organization accepts Medicaid, Medicare, and most major plans.

### Observed strengths and possible conversational opportunity

- BrightView’s website has unusually clear access options and patient education, which supports conversion.
- A high-intent visitor may still have a compound operational question about location, availability, insurance, telehealth, work schedule, or what happens on day one before they are ready to call or select an appointment slot.
- A governed conversation could connect those questions to BrightView’s existing call, schedule, walk-in, or location workflow. This is an inference from public pages, not an assertion of a current operational problem.

## Why BrightView should care about Tars

Tars can add a **strictly non-clinical, governed** conversation before BrightView’s existing intake process:

- Answer only approved questions about locations, appointment process, insurance, payment, and what to expect.
- Gather minimal routing context such as state/ZIP, desired location, preferred contact method, insurance/self-pay, and scheduling preference.
- Route to online scheduling, the right center, or a human admissions/call workflow with the context preserved.
- Support immediate human and emergency/crisis escalation rules; do not provide diagnosis, medication advice, treatment eligibility, or medical direction.

The test is whether more high-intent visitors take an appropriate existing next step—and whether the admissions team receives a better-prepared inquiry.
