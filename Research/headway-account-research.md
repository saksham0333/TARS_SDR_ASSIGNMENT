# Headway — Account Research

**Territory:** Behavioral Health
**Use case:** Customer acquisition
**Verified contacts:** Rutger Flohil, Chief Revenue Officer; Jake Poses, VP, Head of Product (PM, Design, Product Marketing)
**Research date:** 2026-07-29

## What Headway does

Headway is a managed marketplace connecting clients with independent therapists and psychiatrists who accept insurance. It was founded in 2019 after its founder discovered roughly 70% of therapists don't accept insurance, and built a platform to make insurance-based practice administratively viable for independent clinicians.

## Scale and market position

- **60,000+ in-network providers** across all 50 states (up from 34,000 in late 2024 and 26,000 in late 2023 — very rapid network growth).
- **70+ insurance plans**, including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield (select states), Cigna, Oscar, Oxford, United Healthcare, and Optum affiliates.
- Powers **600,000+ therapy appointments per month**; average wait time 4.9 days for therapy, 3.8 days for medication management.
- $325M+ raised to date, $2.3B valuation after a $100M Series D (2024).
- Introduced integrated PCP-referral capability in May 2025 (physicians refer patients directly into Headway via EHR); early pilots across 15,000 physicians produced 10,000 referrals.
- As of July 1, 2026, Headway now confirms a client's location at the time of scheduling for telehealth sessions — if the assigned therapist isn't licensed in that state, the session becomes out-of-network and can't be processed through insurance. This is a new, potentially confusing point in the booking flow.

## Customer experience today

### Consumer journey

1. A client enters ZIP code, insurance carrier, and preferences (gender, language, specialty).
2. Headway shows real-time insurance eligibility and exact copay before booking.
3. Client books directly, typically within about 5 days.
4. As of July 2026, location is re-confirmed at scheduling — a new check that could surface unexpected out-of-network situations for clients who've moved or travel between states.

### Observed acquisition strengths

- Real-time, exact copay calculation removes a major source of pre-booking anxiety in this category.
- Very large, fast-growing provider network reduces "no one available near me" friction.
- New PCP-referral channel shows Headway is actively building non-search acquisition paths, not just relying on direct search traffic.

### Where a conversational layer may help

This is an inference from public information, not an assertion of a problem:

- The new July 2026 location-confirmation-at-scheduling rule is a fresh, specific friction point: a client who was accurately matched at signup could still hit a surprise out-of-network message at the scheduling step if their location doesn't match what was originally entered. A conversational flow can proactively surface and explain this rather than let it appear as a late, confusing rejection.
- With 70+ insurance plans and state-by-state licensing rules, a visitor's compound situation (specific plan, specific state, specific provider licensure) is exactly the kind of multi-variable question a filter-based search may not fully resolve on the first pass.
- The new PCP-referral channel is a distinct, less self-directed audience (referred by a doctor, not self-searching) who may need more guided onboarding than a typical direct-search visitor.

## Why Headway should care about Tars

Tars can support a **strictly non-clinical, governed** first conversation:

- Answer approved questions about insurance eligibility, copay estimation, and the new location/licensing requirement — proactively, before a client hits it as a surprise.
- Collect ZIP/state, insurance plan, service interest (therapy vs. psychiatry/medication management), and preference details.
- Route into Headway's existing real-time eligibility and matching system.
- Provide a distinct, more guided path for the new PCP-referred audience.
- Escalate immediately to human support or crisis resources where appropriate.
