## What ERC Pathlight does

Eating Recovery Center (ERC) treats eating disorders and related co-occurring conditions. Pathlight Mood & Anxiety Center treats primary mood, anxiety, trauma-related, and related conditions. Together, ERC Pathlight provides a national continuum from inpatient and residential care through PHP, IOP, and virtual intensive outpatient care.

## Size and footprint

- LinkedIn lists ERC at **1,001–5,000 employees** and shows roughly **1,730 visible employees**.
- ERC Pathlight’s May 2024 CEO announcement cited **2,000 teammates**, nearly **9,000 patients annually**, and **30+ centers** nationwide at that time.
- The company offers in-person and virtual care, with admissions and insurance support for patients, families, and referring professionals.
- The company’s professional-facing site cites **200+ peer-reviewed studies** and outcomes supporting **30,000+ patients**.

## Customer experience today

### Patient / family journey

1. A visitor explores condition, age, care-level, location, virtual-care, insurance, and treatment information.
2. They call, schedule a conversation, submit a web form, use social media messenger, or connect through a referring provider.
3. ERC describes a four-step path: intro call, free clinical assessment with a Master’s-level clinician, insurance confirmation, and treatment start—potentially in as little as 72 hours once approved.
4. The admissions and insurance teams answer questions, conduct assessment, verify benefits, and recommend the appropriate program.
5. Referring professionals can use online, phone, email, or fax referral paths and receive progress and discharge-planning updates.

### Observed strengths

- Multiple access routes, an explicit admissions process, and seven-day availability.
- A national continuum that lets patients step up or down with one multidisciplinary team.
- Strong insurance support and a dedicated Patient Access Navigator (PAN) model for referral coordination.

### Likely friction worth validating

These are public-site observations, not claims about ERC operations:

- A patient, caregiver, or referrer may arrive with multiple non-clinical questions about program availability, age eligibility, virtual versus in-person care, location/travel, coverage, and what happens next.
- The site offers several contact routes and care pathways. A conversational guide could make the right human path clearer before a visitor abandons or repeats basic details.
- Clinical assessment and level-of-care matching rightly remain human-led. The potential role for Tars is therefore a governed **pre-assessment, non-clinical** conversation.

## Why ERC Pathlight should care about Tars

Tars can support approved, non-clinical conversations across web and supported channels:

- Answer approved questions about programs, eligibility, virtual/in-person options, locations, insurance process, referrals, and next steps.
- Capture limited routing context—visitor type, general age band, state/ZIP, program interest, coverage category, and preferred follow-up—without attempting clinical assessment.
- Direct people to the existing assessment, admissions, insurance, or professional-referral workflow, with a brief structured handoff summary.
- Escalate immediately to ERC Pathlight’s human team and emergency guidance when a visitor needs clinical or urgent help.

For Goran, this is a growth and access hypothesis: help more high-intent patients, families, and referral partners reach the appropriate existing human path while protecting the compassionate, clinically led admission experience. Any deployment should be reviewed by ERC Pathlight’s clinical, privacy, compliance, and security teams, with appropriate HIPAA safeguards where applicable.
