## What Rogers does

Rogers Behavioral Health is a nationally recognized nonprofit provider of specialized psychiatric and addiction treatment. It treats children, teens, and adults for OCD and anxiety, addiction, depression and other mood disorders, eating disorders, trauma, and PTSD through inpatient, residential, partial-hospitalization, intensive-outpatient, and outpatient programs.

## Scale and market position

- Nonprofit founded in 1907; **1,001–5,000 employees** on LinkedIn.
- Locations in **10 states** as of January 2026: California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Minnesota, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Washington, and Wisconsin.
- Rogers positions itself around specialized, evidence-based and measurement-based care; its regional network includes inpatient hospitals, residential programs, and outpatient/PHP/IOP offerings.
- Rogers is actively expanding access, including new residential treatment centers.

## Customer experience today

### Observed consumer journey

1. A prospective patient, family member, or referring professional can explore specialized conditions, populations, levels of care, locations, and insurance information on the Rogers website.
2. The location page explicitly helps visitors who are unsure which Rogers location is the best fit.
3. Visitors can call admissions for an immediate screening or reach out online.
4. Rogers’ admissions team conducts the intake and the appropriate clinical team determines treatment fit, program, and next steps.

### Strengths and opportunity

- The public experience is clear, compassionate, and oriented toward specialized care.
- A person may still have a complex pre-admissions question that spans condition, age, location, program level, insurance, and timing—before they know whether to call a specific location or admissions.
- A governed conversational layer can support that operational moment, then direct the person to Rogers’ existing care-coordination/admissions path. This is an inference from public pages, not a claim about a current Rogers process gap.

## Why Rogers should care about Tars

Tars can operate as a **strictly non-clinical, governed** access layer:

- Answer approved questions about programs, locations, age groups, admissions process, and insurance/payment information.
- Gather minimum routing context: location/state, age pathway, general care interest, insurance/self-pay, and preferred contact method.
- Direct the visitor to current Rogers admissions, care-coordination, location, or online-contact workflows with a structured summary.
- Escalate immediately for crisis, emergency, and out-of-scope questions; never diagnose, determine level of care, decide eligibility, or provide treatment advice.

The goal is to make the first step feel more personal and make the existing human handoff more complete—not to automate specialized clinical care.
