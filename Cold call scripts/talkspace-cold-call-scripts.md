# Talkspace — Cold-Call Scripts

## Katelyn Watson — Chief Marketing Officer

### Opening

> Hi Katelyn, this is [Your Name] with Tars. I know this call is out of nowhere — can I take 30 seconds to explain why I called, and you can decide if it's relevant?

### Reason for the call

> I've been looking at Talkspace's onboarding path — the pre-account insurance check is a strong trust move most competitors don't offer. The part I was curious about is the intake questionnaire right after it, since your own user research shows a meaningful share of people find it long. Given you lead marketing and think about this in MVP terms, I thought it'd be worth a call.

### Value proposition

> Tars gives behavioral-health platforms a HIPAA-ready agent for that non-clinical intake conversation — same information Talkspace already collects, gathered incrementally instead of as one questionnaire, then handed to your existing matching engine. It doesn't touch matching or clinical decisions.

### Discovery questions

Use two:

1. Is intake-questionnaire length something your team already tracks against completion rate?
2. How do you currently test changes to onboarding — as full launches, or smaller MVPs first?
3. Does a visitor's payor type (individual insurance, EAP, self-pay) get resolved before or during the questionnaire today?
4. Is tightening onboarding friction a stated priority for this year, or is it more product-owned right now?

### Close

> If that's a real opportunity, could we get 20 minutes with the right product and growth counterparts? I'd want to scope a narrow, single-entry-point pilot rather than propose anything broad.

### Objection handling

**"We already have a matching engine that works well."**

> Agreed, and I'm not proposing we touch it. This sits one step earlier — how the inputs to that engine get collected — which is a separate, testable question.

**"We don't want AI making mental-health decisions."**

> Completely agree, and that's outside scope. This is strictly non-clinical: payor type, service interest, format preference — with everything clinical still handled by Talkspace's existing process.

## Melissa Velasco — Director, Growth Marketing

### Opening

> Hi Melissa, this is [Your Name] with Tars — cold call, I know. 30 seconds to explain why I called, and you tell me if it's relevant?

### Reason for the call

> I was looking at Talkspace's funnel: strong pre-account insurance check, then an intake questionnaire that some users find too long, based on your own survey data. In a paid-acquisition funnel, that step is where a lot of spend quietly leaks out. Given your performance-marketing background, thought you'd want to know.

### Value proposition

> Tars replaces that intake questionnaire with a conversational flow — same fields, better pacing — integrated with your existing GA and paid-tracking setup, so any lift in completed intake shows up directly in your own attribution, not a separate dashboard.

### Discovery questions

Use two:

1. Do you track completed-intake rate by paid channel or campaign today?
2. Where in the funnel do you see the sharpest drop-off between click and completed intake?
3. Is intake-form performance something your team has tested before, or has it mostly stayed static?
4. Would a single campaign or landing page be a clean way to test this without disrupting the broader funnel?

### Close

> Would 15 minutes be worth it to see if the math holds on one campaign or landing page? I can work around your calendar.

### Objection handling

**"We already have a strong paid program."**

> That's exactly why this matters — a strong program makes every point of intake-conversion lift worth more, since you're already paying for the traffic.

**"Sounds like a chatbot — we get pitched those constantly."**

> Fair pushback. Most of those are support widgets. This replaces the intake form itself and is measured on conversion rate, not engagement.

### If voicemail

> Hi Melissa, [Your Name] from Tars — we help behavioral-health platforms convert more paid traffic by turning the intake questionnaire into a conversation instead of a static form. I'll follow up by email — talk soon.
