# Headway — Cold-Call Scripts

## Rutger Flohil — Chief Revenue Officer

### Opening

> Hi Rutger, this is [Your Name] with Tars. I know this is unexpected — 30 seconds to explain why I called, then you decide if it's relevant?

### Reason for the call

> I've been looking at Headway's new location-confirmation-at-scheduling requirement from July 1st — sensible for compliance, but it also means a client accurately matched at signup could still hit a surprise out-of-network message at booking. Given your background scaling the growth function since 2020, I thought you'd want to know how that might be landing on both sides of the marketplace.

### Value proposition

> Tars gives marketplaces like Headway a HIPAA-ready, non-clinical intake agent that confirms location and insurance details upfront — before matching — so the match that gets made is one that can actually be booked, protecting both client experience and provider time.

### Discovery questions

Use two:

1. Since July, are you seeing more scheduling-stage drop-off tied to location mismatches?
2. How is that friction currently measured — support tickets, churn, or something else?
3. Does growth have visibility into wasted provider-held time slots from mismatches like this?
4. Would one region be a reasonable scope for testing a fix?

### Close

> If that's a real cost, could we get 20 minutes with the right product and ops counterparts? I'd scope this to one region first.

### Objection handling

**"Our matching system already handles this."**

> The matching itself sounds solid — this is really about whether location gets confirmed early enough to prevent the mismatch from surfacing at scheduling instead of at intake.

**"This is a pretty new policy — too early to know if it's a problem."**

> Fair, and that's actually the best time to test something small before the pattern (or the workaround) becomes entrenched.

## Jake Poses — VP, Head of Product

### Opening

> Hi Jake, this is [Your Name] with Tars — cold call, I know. 30 seconds to explain why I called?

### Reason for the call

> I've been looking at Headway's new location-confirmation-at-scheduling rule from July 1st. Given your product background at Thumbtack and LinkedIn, I'd guess you think about exactly this kind of edge case — where a compliance-driven check surfaces late and creates avoidable friction. Wanted to see if product has looked at moving that check earlier.

### Value proposition

> Tars runs a governed, non-clinical intake conversation that confirms location and key details upfront, before matching — so the location check becomes a smooth part of intake instead of a late scheduling-stage surprise.

### Discovery questions

Use two:

1. Has product already scoped a fix for the location-mismatch edge case, or is it still new enough to be unaddressed?
2. How are you currently onboarding the new PCP-referred audience — same flow as self-search clients, or something distinct?
3. Where does location/eligibility confirmation currently live in the intake flow?
4. Would a single region or the PCP-referral channel be a cleaner place to pilot a fix?

### Close

> Would 20 minutes be worth it to scope a pilot around either the location-check moment or the PCP-referral onboarding?

### Objection handling

**"We're still gathering data on the July change — too soon to act."**

> Understood — even so, a small pilot in one region now would give you real data sooner than waiting for the pattern to show up in support volume.

**"Sounds like a support/ops fix, not a product one."**

> It could live in either, but since it's really about when a question gets asked in the flow, product usually owns that lever most directly.

### If voicemail

> Hi Jake, [Your Name] from Tars — we help healthcare marketplaces catch location and eligibility mismatches earlier in the intake flow instead of at scheduling. I'll follow up by email — talk soon.
