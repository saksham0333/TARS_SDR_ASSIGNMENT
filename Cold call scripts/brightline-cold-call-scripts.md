# Brightline — Cold-Call Scripts

## Elizabeth Hart — Chief Marketing Officer

### Opening

> Hi Elizabeth, this is [Your Name] with Tars. I know this is unexpected — 30 seconds to explain why I called, then you tell me if it's relevant?

### Reason for the call

> I've been looking at Brightline's move from virtual-only into hybrid care, market by market. That's a great growth story, but it also means a parent's question — "is in-person available where I am" — doesn't have a single, stable answer right now. Given your acquisition and retention background at Cigna and Eleanor Health, I thought this would be relevant.

### Value proposition

> Tars gives family mental health companies a HIPAA-ready agent for that non-clinical first conversation — clarifying who the appointment is for, the concern, insurance, and format preference, and giving an accurate, centrally maintained answer on local availability, rather than relying on a static page to stay current across every market.

### Discovery questions

Use two:

1. How is coverage/availability information currently kept accurate as new markets go live?
2. Do you see funnel drop-off differ between established markets and newly launched ones?
3. Is the "who is this appointment for" distinction (parent vs. child) something your current funnel handles cleanly?
4. Would testing this alongside your next market launch be a reasonable scope?

### Close

> If that's a real gap, could we get 20 minutes with the team managing your expansion rollout? I'd want to scope this to one upcoming market launch rather than a broad change.

### Objection handling

**"We already communicate availability clearly."**

> That's good to hear — the question I'd ask is how quickly that communication updates when a new market goes live, versus how quickly marketing starts driving traffic there.

**"We don't want AI involved in anything child-related."**

> Understood, and to be clear the scope here is strictly non-clinical — logistics, coverage, and routing only, with immediate escalation to your team for anything beyond that.

## Kelsey Mellard — Chief Growth Officer

### Opening

> Hi Kelsey, this is [Your Name] with Tars — I know this is out of the blue. 30 seconds to explain why I called?

### Reason for the call

> I've been following Brightline's expansion into hybrid care. Given your background at Sitka and in HHS innovation, I'd guess you see growth and access as the same problem — and that's exactly the tension in the funnel right now: keeping "what's available to my family" accurate as markets launch on different timelines.

### Value proposition

> Tars runs a governed, non-clinical intake conversation — clarifying who the appointment is for, the concern, insurance, and format — and keeps availability answers centrally current, so it can move at the same pace as your rollout calendar.

### Discovery questions

Use two:

1. How do you currently manage the gap between a market launch and updated funnel messaging?
2. Is there a metric today for funnel performance in newly launched markets specifically?
3. Would piloting something alongside your next launch be operationally realistic?
4. Who owns the handoff between expansion planning and marketing/funnel updates?

### Close

> Would 20 minutes be worth it to scope a pilot tied to your next market launch specifically?

### Objection handling

**"We're deep in expansion planning — bad timing for anything new."**

> That's actually the ideal timing — testing this alongside a launch means you get a clean before/after comparison instead of retrofitting it later.

**"This feels like a marketing tool, not a growth one."**

> It touches both, which is why I wanted growth in the conversation — the value only shows up if it keeps pace with expansion, not just marketing spend.

### If voicemail

> Hi Kelsey, [Your Name] from Tars — we help healthcare companies keep their acquisition funnel accurate as they expand market by market. I'll follow up by email — talk soon.
