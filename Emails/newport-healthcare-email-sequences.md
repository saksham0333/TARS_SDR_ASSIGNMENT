### Email 3 — Day 8

**Subject:** A small, safe pilot idea for Newport

Hi Kristin,

I would not propose a generic site-wide chatbot.

For Newport, I would test a single high-intent page with an agent that:

1. answers only approved program, age, location, payment, and process questions;
2. gathers non-clinical routing details such as age band, state, insurance category, and preferred follow-up;
3. sends the visitor to the existing admissions or referral workflow; and
4. escalates immediately to human support and Newport’s emergency guidance when needed.

Success would be measured by qualified handoffs and reduced repeat discovery—not chat volume.
