### Email 3 — Day 8

**Subject:** A small, safe ERC Pathlight pilot

Hi Goran,

I would not recommend a generic chatbot across the site.

For ERC Pathlight, I would test one high-intent page with an agent that:

1. answers only approved program, location, virtual-care, insurance, and process questions;
2. gathers minimal non-clinical routing context, such as visitor type, age band, state, and preferred follow-up;
3. routes to the current assessment, admissions, insurance, or referral path; and
4. escalates clearly to human support and emergency guidance when required.

The measure is qualified human handoffs and less repeated discovery—not chat volume.

Worth pressure-testing with patient access, clinical, and compliance leaders?
